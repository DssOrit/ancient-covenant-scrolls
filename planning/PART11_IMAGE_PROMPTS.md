# Load Survival: Part 11 photographs (24) and four replacement Part 8 covers

Rules for every image: realistic documentary photograph, 16:9 landscape, 1600x900 or smaller, JPG, under 250 KB where possible, NO words, numbers, captions, signs, labels or logos (use plain packaging, blank paper, coloured tape instead of labels), no repeated image, same characters throughout each story, one clear safe action per frame. Send back in small ZIP batches of 3 or 4 JPGs, named exactly as shown.

Never show: climbing unsafe ladders or storm-damaged trees, bare or twisted wires, a battery in sun or swollen, a person on a bamboo platform or raft, sharpened stakes or traps, a person drinking rainwater or bamboo liquid, floodwater used as supply, open flames in an enclosed space.

## Characters
- Story A (orchard): a woman in her 50s, grey-streaked hair, linen shirt, straw hat, and an older man in a plain work shirt, in a small Portuguese-style fruit garden.
- Story B (bamboo): a man in his 40s, safety glasses, thick gloves, long sleeves, sturdy boots.
- Story C (water): a woman in her 30s, green rain jacket, a boy about 10 in a yellow raincoat.
- Story D (solar): a teenager in a plain hoodie and a woman in a cardigan, at a garden table.
