# Part 11E (traditional bamboo fishing): 6 picture prompts

Same rules as before: realistic documentary photograph, 16:9, 1600x900 or smaller, JPG, under 250 KB where possible, NO words, numbers, labels or logos, no repeated image, same person in all six (a man in his 40s, safety glasses, thick gloves, long sleeves, boots, plain cap). One safe action per frame. Send back as one ZIP of 3 and one ZIP of 3, named exactly as shown.

Never show: wading in a current, a person holding a sharpened bamboo spear or point toward a fish or person, a gorge hook swallowed by a fish, a trap being set in water, floodwater, or any method as a beginner how-to.
