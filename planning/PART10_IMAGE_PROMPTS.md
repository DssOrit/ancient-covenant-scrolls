Rules for every image: realistic documentary photograph, 16:9 landscape, 1600x900 or smaller, JPG, under 250 KB where possible, NO words, numbers, captions, signs, labels or logos anywhere in the picture (use blank or blurred paper, plain bottles, colour tape instead of labels), no repeated image, same characters throughout each story, one clear safe action per frame.
