# Load Survival: remaining pictures (flood stories and Part 8 stories)

MASTER STYLE (paste first in a new chat): Make a realistic documentary-style colour photograph, wide 16:9 landscape, 1600 x 900 pixels, natural soft light, muted colours. NO words, letters, numbers, signs, labels, logos or brand names anywhere in the picture (use blank paper, plain packaging or coloured tape instead). One clear, safe action per picture. Keep the same people, clothes and look for every picture in the same story. Never show: anyone wading in or touching floodwater, flames or candles in a room, a person on a roof or in an attic, windows or doors sealed with plastic or tape, towels or rags in drains, bare or live wires, a generator indoors, or anything unsafe shown as the right way. If any text, number or logo appears, redo it with none. Give each as a JPG, 1600 x 900, under 250 KB.

HOW TO USE: 1) Paste the MASTER STYLE. 2) Upload the reference picture for the story (from your earlier zip or the app) and say: "Use the same people and style." 3) Paste one prompt per message. 4) Save each picture with the filename shown. You can ask for two at once side by side, as before. Send me the JPGs (or 2 x 1 / 2 x 2 sheets) and the phrase "edit Load Survival".
