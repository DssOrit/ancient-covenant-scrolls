Why Sunrise to Sunrise

The ancient Hebrew solar day runs from morning to morning. The Book of Yovelim states in the Orit Ge’ez that the day runs from morning to the following day at even-tide. Bereshit 1 in the DSS establishes light came first — day preceded night. Sunset-to-sunset follows the Babylonian lunisolar calendar adopted by Rabbinic tradition after the exile. The DSS community and the Orit Ge’ez preserved the original solar reckoning.

Source: Yovelim 2:9 — Damascus Document CD Columns 10-11 — 4QCalendrical Texts 4Q320-330
